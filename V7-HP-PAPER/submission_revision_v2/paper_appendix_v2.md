# Appendix: Reader-Safe Context Action Selection

## A. Metric definitions

The completed run has generated answers and document-level contexts but no predicted supporting sentence IDs. We therefore report custom title-level evidence metrics.

- **Title recall@5:** fraction of gold supporting document titles present in the selected five titles.
- **Title F1:** title-set precision/recall F1 under the archived implementation, including its full-recall adjustment.
- **Answer-title product:** per-query answer token F1 multiplied by title F1, then averaged.

Legacy artifacts use `sp_f1` for title F1 and `joint_f1` for the product. Neither is the official HotpotQA supporting-fact or joint metric. Gold sentence labels recovered in `gold_supporting_sentence_labels.jsonl` are provided for provenance and a future sentence-prediction evaluation only.

## B. Data construction

The main data source is Hugging Face `hotpot_qa`, `distractor`, validation. All 7,405 rows pass the archived normalization condition. A local `random.Random(44)` shuffles the candidate IDs, and the first 1,000 form the evaluation set. Re-execution matches the stored ID order exactly.

The data checksum is `fba436e504e84d0180058fd0035884f53d4930f3c7a7ba9b6ce2dcf06acf7d59`; the metadata checksum is `4dbf71130b5afa4ee66952d56241c4df62d1b3d804f7ef40be72d8232ea49190`. All IDs and fold memberships are included in the machine-readable manifests.

## C. Fully nested protocol

For each outer fold:

1. Sort query IDs by integer MD5 and hold out one 200-query slice.
2. Use only the remaining 800 queries to construct action outcomes and fit configuration choices.
3. Divide outer train into five inner query folds.
4. Fit the answer-safety nuisance model on four inner folds and predict the fifth.
5. Concatenate inner-OOF predictions as the outer-train safety feature.
6. Fit a nuisance model on all 800 outer-train queries and predict the 200 outer-test queries.
7. Select model type, action families, thresholds, and coverage on outer-train OOF estimates.
8. Freeze the entire pipeline and emit outer-test actions without outcome feedback.

The feature audit verifies zero train/test overlap in every outer and inner split. Every fold records `outer_test_outcome_used=false`.

## G. Utility-weight sensitivity

The inherited utility uses fixed product weight 1.0 and sweeps:

- answer weight: 0.5, 0.8, 1.0;
- title-recall weight: 0.1, 0.3, 0.5;
- title-F1 weight: 0.1, 0.2, 0.3.

All 27 combinations are trained and configured without held-out weight selection. Across their concatenated outer-test diagnostics:

- answer-F1 delta: -0.0073 to +0.0001;
- product delta: +0.0042 to +0.0117;
- title-recall delta: +0.0130 to +0.0165;
- title-F1 delta: +0.0169 to +0.0219;
- selected answer-drop rate: 0.060 to 0.070.

The scalar utility is consistently evidence-seeking but not reliably answer-preserving. This motivates the weight-free two-stage primary objective.

## I. Failure and opportunity taxonomy

Failures should be separated into:

1. **No positive main-eligible action (797 queries):** candidate-generation failure; selector cannot recover.
2. **Positive action available but missed:** ranking/classification failure.
3. **Positive action selected (59 selected actions):** realized action-level success under the paper label.
4. **Selected answer drop (29 actions):** reader-safety failure.
5. **Fallback despite opportunity:** selective-coverage or threshold failure.

These categories can overlap under different diagnostic definitions, so main figures must state their priority assignment. The 797 count is a query-level opportunity statistic and should not be mixed with selected-action counts without a defined partition.

## K. Federated-scope audit

Documents receive synthetic round-robin client IDs after sanitization. Candidate text is centralized, and the organizer does not execute secure aggregation, differential privacy, communication budgeting, or non-IID client training. Upstream FL/HP-hyper experiments motivate why routing changes can be flattened; they do not establish an end-to-end federated result for this selector.

## L. External 2Wiki diagnostic

The frozen action/selector design was connected to a 300-query 2WikiMultiHopQA development adapter. Positive-action availability and oracle opportunity remained larger than deployable gains, while the transferred selector produced near-zero product improvement. This is a failure boundary: the Hotpot-derived action templates and features do not establish cross-dataset generalization.

## M. Proxy baselines

Repository rows named SetR-style, RankRAG-style, or Influence-style use simple local scoring rules over the same candidate table. They are conceptual controls, not executions of the cited systems. They are excluded from the main table and support no “outperforms” claim.

## N. Reproduction commands

The exact commands are packaged in `run_commands.sh`. In order:

1. `nested_safe_answer_feature_generation.py` rebuilds nested nuisance features and split audits;
2. `nested_selector_training.py` creates main decisions, significance, ablations, risk coverage, utility sensitivity, and action-scope statistics;
3. `build_submission_manifests.py` reconstructs source provenance, folds, recovered gold sentence labels, environment, and checksums;
4. `generate_risk_figure.py` renders the diagnostic PDF.

The packaging environment uses Python 3.10.20, PyTorch 2.3.1+cu121, Transformers 4.41.2, Datasets 4.8.5, scikit-learn 1.7.2, NumPy 1.26.4, SciPy 1.15.3, and Matplotlib 3.10.9. Four A100-PCIE-40GB GPUs were available, though the nested selector rerun itself is CPU-only. Primary paired bootstrap uses 2,000 resamples and seed 13.
