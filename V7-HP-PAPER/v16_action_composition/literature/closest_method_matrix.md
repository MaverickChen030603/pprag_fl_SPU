## Contract conclusions

1. Several works are multi-step, but the multi-step object is usually retrieval, decomposition, or variable-size subset construction rather than edits to a fixed five-document context.
2. Pair and contextual-utility work already invalidate any broad claim that V16 is the first to model passage interactions.
3. Context-Picker makes “RL-based context selection” and “minimal sufficient subset” unavailable as novelty claims.
4. The defensible hypothesis is narrower: under a frozen pool and fixed cardinality, a state-dependent edit trajectory can realize reader gains beyond every legal single edit, measured by preregistered strict synergy.
5. Numerical comparisons are permitted only when official contracts can be matched on pool, context budget, reader, and test-time calls; otherwise the work belongs in a contract matrix, not the main results table.
