# V18 Phase Contract

| Checkpoint | Question | Entry rule | Allowed outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Do natural silos create Bc-realizable reader-context opportunity? | frozen V17 N=100 audit passes | strong pass, conditional pass, or fail |
| A2 | Is a high-dispersion slow path warranted? | conditional A only | targeted method or redirect |
| B | Can a centralized action model realize oracle opportunity? | strong/confirmed conditional A | learnable or not learnable |
| C | Does federated personalization retain centralized utility? | B learnable | effective or training gap large |
| D | Can reader-aligned selective upload lower training payload? | C effective | effective or unnecessary |

The primary comparison at B/C is the strongest budget-matched
non-compositional Fast Path. Every row must match query IDs, Bc, local-k,
candidate count, final K, reader, partition, and latency protocol. The primary
quality endpoint is dataset-native Joint/composite F1; all secondary tests use
paired bootstrap confidence intervals, paired p-values, and BH adjustment.

V18's future opportunity-detector state may use only router score distributions,
client-score entropy, top-client margin, local-score statistics, entity overlap,
bridge features, redundancy, client diversity, disagreement, candidate-order
instability, baseline-context confidence, question complexity, and predicted
hop/dispersion signals. It may not use gold support-client membership, gold
answers, answer presence, target-reader outcomes, or final labels at inference.
