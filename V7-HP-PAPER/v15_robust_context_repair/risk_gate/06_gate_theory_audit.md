# Gate Theory Audit

## Implemented claim boundary

`05_risk_controlled_gate.py` evaluates a finite, frozen threshold family on an
independent calibration split. It applies one-sided exact binomial upper bounds
to selected Answer-drop and Joint-drop rates and a bounded Hoeffding lower bound
to mean Answer delta. A Bonferroni correction covers all threshold/risk tests.

The resulting statement is valid only under all of these conditions:

1. calibration queries and deployment queries are exchangeable;
2. no scorer, candidate generator, threshold grid, reader, prompt, or metric is
   changed after calibration labels are inspected;
3. each query is the unit of sampling and selection is independent per query;
4. the calibration actions and their outcomes were generated by the same frozen
   pipeline used at deployment;
5. reported coverage is not guaranteed and may collapse to exact fallback;
6. distribution shift, subgroup guarantees, reader changes, and adaptive reuse
   of calibration data are outside the guarantee.

Until these conditions pass the artifact audit, manuscript wording must remain
"empirically calibrated per-query risk gate." Passing the code alone is not
sufficient to claim a finite-sample or distribution-free guarantee.
